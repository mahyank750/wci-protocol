> A capture-resistant, non-compensatory wellbeing index and reference unit of account designed to align economic metrics with family stability, health, peace, ecology, and freedom.

## 🌍 Overview
The World Commonwealth Index (WCI) proposes a unified reference unit of account rooted in human wellbeing rather than purely financial output. It addresses structural limitations in traditional economic systems by integrating non-compensatory aggregation rules (geometric mean) and severe multiplier penalties for systemic harms such as family fragmentation, corruption, addiction, and armed conflict.

## 🏛️ Core Pillars
1. **Family & Social Stability (50% Weight - *Open for debate*):** Prioritizing multi-generational households, child-rearing support, and relational permanence.
2. **Health & Longevity:** Universal access to preventative care, mental wellbeing, and public health security.
3. **Peace & Security:** Measures of local and global conflict mitigation, personal safety, and social trust.
4. **Ecological Stewardship:** Sustainable resource utilization, carbon accountability, and biodiversity protection.
5. **Civil Freedom & Governance:** Protection of individual rights, transparent governance, and resistance to institutional capture.

## ⚖️ Penalty Framework
WCI applies proportional and multiplicative penalties (quadratic factors) for severe societal dysfunctions:
- Family breakdown and domestic instability
- Systemic corruption and bribery
- Substance addiction epidemics
- Aggressive warfare and state-sponsored violence
